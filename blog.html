How to Choose a Reliable Fitness Clothing Manufacturer for Your Brand

📅 October 14, 2026 👤 By KUNDAO Team 📂 Manufacturer Guide

Starting a fitness clothing brand is exciting, but one of the biggest challenges is finding a reliable manufacturer. The right supplier can make or break your brand. In this guide, we'll walk you through the key factors to consider when choosing a gym wear manufacturer.

1. Check Fabric Quality First

Fabric is the foundation of any good fitness apparel. Always ask for samples before committing to a bulk order. Pay attention to:

  • Stretch and recovery: Good workout fabric should stretch and bounce back without losing shape.
  • Breathability: Look for mesh panels or moisture-wicking fabrics.
  • Durability: Check if the fabric pills after a few washes.

2. Understand MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity)

If you're a new brand, a high MOQ can be risky. Look for manufacturers who offer flexible MOQ options. At KUNDAO Apparel, our starting MOQ is just 50 pieces per design, making it easier for new brands to test the market.

3. Always Order Samples First

Never skip the sample step! A sample lets you:

  • Test the actual fabric quality and feel
  • Check the fit and sizing
  • See the logo printing quality
  • Evaluate the overall craftsmanship

4. Ask About Customization Options

Your brand needs to stand out. Make sure the manufacturer offers:

  • Custom fabric colors and weights
  • Multiple logo printing methods (screen print, embroidery, heat transfer)
  • Custom labels and packaging
  • Size customization for your target market

5. Communication is Key

A reliable manufacturer should respond to your inquiries within 24-48 hours. Clear communication throughout the production process prevents misunderstandings and delays.

Conclusion

Choosing the right fitness clothing manufacturer takes time and research. Don't rush into a partnership with the first supplier you find. Order samples, compare options, and choose a partner who understands your brand vision.
